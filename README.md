# vbm-work

**The vocabulary of work.** A task goes in, a typed outcome comes out, and between them a review returns typed findings. The words two machines share, written once in Lean 4 and machine-checked: the four ways a run ends and the nineteen kinds of block (each with one word, a word naming at most one, a word that names none refused), what makes an outcome well formed, what a reviewer may return, and what a round's review comes to (a `fix` blocks, notes never do, an unanswered reviewer is never an approval).

> **Status: pre-release.** The first slice only. Nothing is published to a package registry.

## What it is, and what it is not

A specification and its proofs, over `Init` alone: no input or output, no wire format, no dependency. A machine that schedules work and a machine that carries one task through its cycle both depend on it and on nothing of each other's. It decides nothing about what to do next; reading a reply into findings or writing an outcome as JSON belongs to the application that binds it.

## Build and test

Prerequisites: `git`, [mise](https://mise.jdx.dev). `mise exec -- scripts/tier1.sh` is the gate (Lean 4.35.0, pinned in `mise.toml`). The claims and the theorems that prove them are in `docs/proof-ledger.md`, generated by the gate.

## How it is built

Agents write it under mechanical gates instead of line-by-line review, to the [VBM standard](https://github.com/witt3rd/vbm). It lands directly on `main` with `.land/land-direct`, a pinned copy of [vbm-land](https://github.com/witt3rd/vbm-land).
